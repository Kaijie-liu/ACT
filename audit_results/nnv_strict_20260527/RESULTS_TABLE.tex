\vspace{0.2em}
\centering\scriptsize
\textbf{Protocol.}\;
Three source patches (see \texttt{patches/}):
(1) \texttt{run\_vnncomp\_instance.m} adds env-gated \texttt{NNV\_STRICT\_NO\_HELPER=1} that
skips \texttt{falsify\_single} entirely (no random sampling, no lb/ub corner eval) and refuses
\texttt{cp-star} reachMethod (conformal prediction, not sound).
(2) \texttt{matlab2nnv.m} R2026a compat shim: \texttt{ScalingLayer} (auto-inserted by
\texttt{importNetworkFromONNX} on R2026a) mapped to existing \texttt{ElementwiseAffineLayer}
($y=Scale\cdot x+Offset$; semantically identical).
(3) \texttt{nnv\_strict\_run\_one.m} parpool isolation (private JobStorageLocation per
MATLAB pid) and \texttt{NNV\_NUMCORES=5} env override to avoid inter-lane parpool thrash.
