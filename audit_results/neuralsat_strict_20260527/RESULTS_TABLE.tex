\vspace{0.2em}
\centering\scriptsize
\textbf{Protocol.}\;
\texttt{-{}-disable\_attack} sets \texttt{Settings.use\_attack=False}, which gates
\texttt{\_pre\_attack} (PGD), \texttt{\_mip\_attack}, and \texttt{\_attack} inside
\texttt{verifier.py}. All SAT verdicts arise from completeness of BaB+bound-propagation,
not gradient-based attack. Dual-lane parallel sweep + 22-instance serial rerun for OOM
diagnosis (3 confirmed OOM-in-parallel: collins\_rul idx 21/22/43; 19 NeuralSAT internal
SIGSEGV on ACOPF: ml4acopf idx 15--19, 43--56).
