\vspace{0.2em}
\centering\scriptsize
\textbf{Protocol.}\;
\texttt{-{}-NOPGD} translates to \texttt{-{}-pgd\_order=skip}, which also auto-disables
\texttt{adv\_warmup}, \texttt{input\_split.check\_adv}, BaB-attack and MIP-attack. All SAT verdicts
arise from completeness of bound-propagation+BaB, not gradient-based attack.
