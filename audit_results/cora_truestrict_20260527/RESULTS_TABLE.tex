\vspace{0.2em}
\centering\scriptsize
\textbf{Headline finding.}\;
Under TRUESTRICT (\texttt{falsification\_method='none'}, all helpers patched out),
CORA's pure over-approximative reachability proves \textbf{only the 2 smallest smoke-test instances}.
Every other benchmark returns \texttt{unknown} (reachable set intersects unsafe region, but cannot
distinguish real counter-examples from over-approximation artifacts). \emph{All of CORA's
competition success is attributable to its falsification helpers, not to its reachability.}

\vspace{0.2em}
\centering\scriptsize
\textbf{Protocol.}\;
Three source patches gate a new \texttt{falsification\_method='none'} option in CORA's
\texttt{validateNNoptions.m}, \texttt{verify.m}, and \texttt{prepare\_instance.m}. This option
does \emph{not} exist in the upstream competition release — we added it to expose what CORA's
reachability does without any falsification heuristic (no \texttt{center}-of-box, no FGSM,
no \texttt{zonotack}, no random sampling). Full diffs in \texttt{patches/}.
