\vspace{0.2em}
\centering\scriptsize
\textbf{Protocol.}\;
\texttt{--STRICT} = \texttt{falsification\_method=center}, \texttt{refinement\_method=naive}.
This disables CORA's three falsification helpers (\texttt{zonotack} vertex sampling,
\texttt{fgsm} gradient step, \texttt{nrSamp} random samples) and the
zonotope-heuristic refinement loop. A verdicts here arise from a \emph{single}
deterministic evaluation at the input-box centre, not from adversarial search.
Compare with the official CORA submission (commit \texttt{6f19230},
arXiv:2512.19007) which uses CORA's full counter-example pipeline. Official
numbers are deduped by (onnx, vnnlib) because CORA's \texttt{results.csv}
prepends a warmup row that duplicates instance \#1 of every benchmark.
