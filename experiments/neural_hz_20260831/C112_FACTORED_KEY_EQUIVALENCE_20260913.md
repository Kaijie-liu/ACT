# C112 complete frame/mask factorization of the same exact quotient

## Identity and complete-domain partition

For a retained channel, bind all active positional triples (position, original
column, dyadic exponent). Normalize the complete frame by the exponent at its
smallest original column. Two complete identical frame keys differ only by a
common power of two. A signed masked T tensor T expression is then identified
by that frame and its normalized positive/negative positional bitsets. The
relative powers and original IDs are in the key, not inferred from the mask.

Distinct disjoint frames cannot have equal nonempty forms. An exact original-ID
overlap index marks BOTH complete frame identities whenever different frames
share a column; internal duplicate columns are marked too. ALL their retained
forms take unchanged C111 full literal normalization, with its full old fee in
addition to C112 binding. Thus no partially overlapping expressions are lost,
and no cross-partition equality is possible. No instance or LP state is used.

Within a fast-path group, the smallest original column in the actual subset
has a fixed relative exponent. Ordering by channel reference therefore selects
the same maximum leading power as C111; the same original-key tie and earliest
member ordering remain. Normalizing sign by position instead of original ID
does not change relative sign within a fixed group. Every original V consumer
gets the same representative/sign/power route. C96 exact row emission and
normalization are copied unchanged; tests compare their function source.

This is a reduction in repeated exact normalization, not a new approximate
domain. C111's source-first omission theorem still supplies unique normalized
auxiliary extensions and the original coordinate inverse. EQ/INEQ, binary
phases, shared latent identities and nonzero original centers/bias remain.

All 15 C96/C111/C112 fixtures compare full auxiliary/output native row tuples,
independent direct spatial polynomials, actual source EQ/INEQ with both binary
phases, all normalized boxes and unique inverse extensions. The saved before/
after numeric archives are byte-identical to all 15 C111 archives. They include
source arrays, bounds, integrality, owners and actual inverse arrays; this is
not the full production Python/LIVE owner graph.

Four additional disjoint/shared/dyadic-shared/partially-overlapping layouts
compare complete route results with C111. Eight ordinary masks check all 16
signed templates against the actual stencil. Default-off/prepaid limits and
unchanged numeric row prices are checked. This does not qualify a new real
