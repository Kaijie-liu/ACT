# Shared curvature forward research resume

Latest work is [a constructive paper rule](definition_first_20260928/d118_shared_curvature_transfer_20261002/THEORY.md) for existing activation groups on or near a common affine segment. The source may have several genuine dimensions; it is not a globally scalar network. For ReLU, common interpolation defects have an exact finite standalone hull described by discrete concavity and one curvature budget. It lowers to m+1 rows and at most 3m+6 coordinate nonzeros with no new variables or bits, before all real latent expansion and proof costs.

The main positive control is stronger than an independent-error-box comparison: a biased two-source tuple passes all ten complete source-labelled two-gate hulls, the tight individual defect caps, discrete concavity, and the next gate with true global scalar bounds, yet the new joint budget excludes its child output by 1/10. Opposing residuals from a third source of size 1/20 preserve a 1/20 separation. These are paper convex-mixture witnesses, not runtime phase enumeration, concrete adversarial examples or numerical solver results.

A common-kernel extension covers smooth curvature with explicitly certified bounds. Its illustrative mixed-readout bound is twice as tight as independent coordinate Taylor remainders; the same shared Peano-kernel reference is exactly equal. The kernel, convex interpolation and group-hull mechanisms are classical. No new-domain novelty, trained-model gain, GPU acceleration or universal nonlinear closure is established.
