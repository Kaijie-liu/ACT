## Hypothesis and unchanged scope

C67's 100965 exact local eliminations use denominator-tagged negative words.
The old native lineage reader misinterprets these as legacy aliases. Keep the
complete externally proved C67 source maps immutable and shared. Store only a
sparse journal of every independently discovered unit splice: column, positive
splice descriptor, offset, retired UID pair and MAIN tail UID updates. Explicit
schemas distinguish the source local edges from the journal's positive tags.
No old Closed receipt or fresh-map ownership permit is manufactured.

For a producer p*x + prefix = offset and its unique consumer -s*p*x + tail,
the same exact row addition replaces the consumer by s*prefix + tail and
deletes the producer. Reconstruction from its surviving continuous prefix is
x = (offset - s*new_prefix)/p. After all unit splices are restored, reconstruct
the source local edges topologically from 2^q*x = numerator*parent. Selected
source local factors have no physical incidences; the full discovery rule
rejects dependencies between selected unit definitions. Original inputs and
binary coordinates are never rewritten. Original source proof supplies box
redundancy; point reconstruction still checks the complete latent box.

The journal is a default-off mathematical primitive, NOT a native admission
certificate. Hashes bind a full independently checked source; self hashes and
caller flags cannot replace its proof. There is no production/runtime hook.
