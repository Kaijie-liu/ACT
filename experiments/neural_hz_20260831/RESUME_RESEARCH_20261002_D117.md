The best possible decoder of those means, even nonlinear, has global worst-case error equal to that approximation error. This is classical approximation/moment duality applied with explicit neural scope, not new general mathematics or a completed Neural-HZ definition.

The result does not apply to decoding a particular state from injective pointwise features and does not impose a lower bound on a full HZ retaining more relations. It concerns convex mixtures admitted by a specified summary. It quantifies a gap already known qualitatively in D051; do not present the qualitative finite-moment obstruction as new work.
