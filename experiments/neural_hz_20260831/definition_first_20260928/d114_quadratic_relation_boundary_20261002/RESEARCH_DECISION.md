## New mathematical decision

The [quadratic boundary theorem](THEORY.md) covers all current-layer original bits, not just output-only polynomials. With the specified feasible transverse pair crossings, every degree-two identity is a constant combination of four single-gate relations. It remains true for an overcomplete bank with more gates than source dimensions. The proof preserves both legal zero-phase labels and leaves all original source predicates in force.

Do not build a new quadratic-ideal kernel or GPU monomial store merely to discover these same relations. This conclusion is narrower than saying all quadratic methods, all dependent sources or all Neural-HZ improvements are useless. Source inequalities, noncrossing support geometry and genuinely cross-layer information are explicitly outside the exclusion. Known RLT/envelope strengthening remains a fair comparator, not a forbidden baseline or an innovation claim.

## A remaining concrete research question

A useful next question is how to transfer a bounded joint amplitude interface when its original phase anchors change across a biased residual block. For the ordinary structure

    u=ReLU(A*x+a)
    v=ReLU(B*u+S*x+b)
    w=ReLU(C*v+T*u+c),

can a forward, structure-triggered, sound update retain useful correlations for several consumers without materializing every new-bit times complete-source direction? All original bits, predicates, source identities and decoder would remain. Explicitly quantified information loss is allowed; arbitrary-depth exactness and global ideality are not added as requirements.

This question is not answered by relabeling the old phase-conditioned tensor. It needs a concrete advantage against the archived fixed-anchor interface, a fresh current-anchor construction and the known cross-phase/source constraints, with source binding, all residual terms, readouts, lifting, terminal queries and evidence costs paid. Current archive results already rule out retaining only phase coexistence masses or only an ordinary low-rank sum. No new anchor-update theorem, prototype, freeze or execution is registered by this note.
