## Other ideas examined but not selected

A general old-four-cell to new-four-cell moment transportation system has a standard capacitated-flow projection. Eliminating its cell amplitudes introduces up to 254 nontrivial cut combinations per observation, while the small cell extension can be much cheaper. No new implementation or general transport solver is justified by that reformulation alone.

A shared residual change-of-anchor quantity was also derived. For q=ReLU(f), qprime=ReLU(f+r), old/new bits alpha,gamma and certified L<=r<=U, the canonical d=(alpha-gamma)*f obeys

```text
0<=d<=max(-L,0)*(1-gamma)+max(U,0)*gamma
q-qprime+L*gamma<=d<=q-qprime+U*gamma.
```

For z_j=a_j*f+rho_j with l_j<=rho_j<=u_j, old M_j=alpha*z_j and new N_j=gamma*z_j,

```text
l_j*gamma-u_j*alpha <= N_j-M_j+a_j*d
                         <= u_j*gamma-l_j*alpha.
```

These follow from selected product relations. Their potential benefit is sharing d among consumers, but no strict comparison against the appropriate existing interfaces was established. They remain an unimplemented secondary derivation, not another runtime path or a competing promoted candidate.

Cross-field sources reinforce the need to retain joint identity. [Jeannet, Gopan and Reps, SAS 2005, section 4.2 and Theorem 1](https://research.cs.wisc.edu/wpis/papers/sas05.pdf) treats preservation of relational properties in abstractions of functions; it does not supply our budget transfer rule. [Leskela and Vihola, Theorems 1.3 and 1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.0999) establish conditional coupling existence. Our inference is that such existence is not a certificate for the original network's same theta. No probabilistic model, stochastic guarantee or martingale shortcut is introduced.
