# Neural HZ research decision and custody

The active objective remains a powerful definition-first nonconvex Neural-HZ, including genuine validation gains and GPU-oriented propagation, not a standalone query helper. The preceding alignment-only turn was no progress. This turn revalidated the actual worktree and D139 archive, completed a constructive forward relation proof and a stronger comparison, and records the result without promotion.

## What changed in the research

The new [theory](THEORY.md) uses one existing nonnegative activation to weight the same source, phase and physical readouts across biased affine layers, mixed ReLUs and live skips. Multiplication by a fixed anchor does not require a growing polynomial degree in retained network coordinates. The original descendant output equation reuses an already-present weighted bit and adds four quotient rows without another variable.

The paper control proves J<=1/3 and a subsequent original activation identically zero. A specified intersection of full individual child source-labelled hulls, even with the complete anchor-phase lift, admits J>=39/100 and a positive successor. Strictly interior real witnesses and epsilon in [1/200,1/100] avoid zero-label or endpoint artifacts. All original labels remain protected nevertheless.

This positive comparison fails the stronger project-reference gate. Plain weighting is dominated by the complete phase lift, and the quotient is already an archived D035 primitive. More decisively, D052's existing fixed coefficient matcher on (q,y+1/2) directly gives J<=-epsilon*(beta+gamma)<=0, using one row, five physical-coordinate nonzeros and no auxiliary. It covers the ENTIRE perturbation family, not only its central point, and explicitly allows the internal frontier and negative constants used here. The new system's seven continuous quantities and up to 74 LE/188 coordinate nonzeros therefore do not justify implementation. No actual network applicability rate or cost advantage has been established.

## Other explored ideas not selected

The phase-reference shared amplitude q=q0+J*a was checked against D012 and D074 and is a reparameterization, not a new candidate. Local D121 tail removal using an actual zero mismatch amplitude is unsafe: x=1/2,y=1/8 in q1=R(x+1/4),q2=R(y-1/2+q1/2) has original phases (1,1), q=(3/4,0), but the first-order phase-series reference is (3/4,-3/8); a nonzero tail must cancel its second coordinate.

For an independent common scalar t in [0,1] and phase set B with known bounded hull P={beta:A*beta<=d}, the joint hull of z=t*beta is exactly z in tP and beta-z in (1-t)P. Endpoint distributions prove both inclusions. Free phases reduce this to the usual jointly ideal star-product envelopes; phase constraints can help, for example sum beta<=1 gives sum z<=t. Source-dependent neural guards destroy the assumed Cartesian independence, so this useful classical perspective factor is not selected as a closed Neural-HZ domain.

An explicit phase-polynomial expansion of a conditional error support can have many terms, but this is not a general query lower bound: for otherwise free phases the support of eta*(d+w*beta), eta in [-epsilon,epsilon], is epsilon*max(abs(d+sum w_positive),abs(d+sum w_negative)). A compact expression and a constrained-source query are different costs. No implementation was justified by the rank argument.

Repeated-ReLU cyclic consistency reduced to existing cross-gap relations. For q_i=R(g_i), n_i=q_i-g_i and a permutation pi, the expression sum q_i*(g_i-g_pi(i)) minus half sum (q_i-q_pi(i))^2 equals sum q_i*n_pi(i)>=0. The corresponding bit cycle is a sum of the D012 cross-gate charges. No new cyclic domain or optimizer was built.

A shared uncertain residual operator can preserve correlations lost by independent approximation errors, but the actual model weights are fixed and known. Such an outer uncertainty model is not evidence of superiority to HZ using those exact weights. That route remains unselected, with unknown constrained-phase and terminal costs.
