## What this changes

The previous paper question now has an executed answer: cross-query information can survive a concrete Softmax-to-PV forward interface and substantially improve its output-difference abstraction. However, the demonstrated gain is already obtained more cheaply and more strongly by a known incremental relation with shared value consumption. It is not evidence for inventing logarithmic-mean mathematics, renaming a graph, or continuing to eliminate its auxiliary variables.

Do not scale this two-token endpoint justification to the full ViT/CIFAR/Tiny populations and call it definition innovation. Retain the implementation and controls as a reusable comparison fixture. Further definition research must demonstrate a compositional or precision/full-cost advantage that survives a stated practical known-relations reference. Larger joint-query/multi-channel structures are a possible next hypothesis, not an approved gain or an excuse to omit the stronger reference. No such larger experiment has been started in this run.

The underlying exact extension proof remains conditional on caller-certified common network semantics. Frame-label checks do not independently bind actual model operations, and literal rationalization of production float coefficients is not an outward-rounding certificate. These are explicit admission requirements before native reuse, not claimed completed work.
