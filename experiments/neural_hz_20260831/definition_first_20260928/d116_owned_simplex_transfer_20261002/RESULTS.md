## What this settles and what it does not

The local owned-face separating example still passes its exact test: the new row excludes that local relaxation point by 1/20. The fixed neural block, however, already contains additional source relations. Local strictness therefore did not translate into a measured downstream improvement. The original gate identity and conditional source bound were preserved, not removed to recover a favorable example.

The result is limited to the declared block and 14 directions. Equality of finitely many support values does not establish equality of the physical projections, and does not prove the six rows redundant in the complete auxiliary system. No unregistered separation query was added after observing the result. The C/E agreement is evidence for the known projection's implementation, not evidence of new precision beyond the known perspective/RLT extension.

This candidate is consequently not promoted. Do not expand this fixture into trained-model, GPU or benchmark runs merely because the component tests pass. Preserve its implementation as a scoped supporting result, not as the main Neural-HZ contribution.

## Implication for the definition research

The unresolved issue is a useful finite relationship language that retains common source identity across nonlinear successors and has a justified compositional cost. Simply adding the missing beta-times-w observation is already a selected product lifting; it is not a new-domain definition. The [earlier observation relay](../d089_observation_relay_20261001/THEORY.md) already explored forward aggregate observations and documented why independent local witnesses can fail to combine. Repackaging that mechanism or the present six-row projection would repeat prior work.

A subsequent hypothesis must identify a structural/compositional contribution beyond those known interfaces and give a fair comparison at full cost before implementation. The current negative result does not require abandoning nonconvex Neural-HZ, reducing the original goal, deleting any original phase, or replacing the domain with a convex abstraction. It does require stopping promotion of this particular no-gain comparison. No replacement candidate or additional solver/model run is registered by this document.
