## What genuine algorithmic work could remove, and what remains unknown

A new birth representation could genuinely avoid materializing selected direct
row buffers and an old Ac merely to compare and replace them. It could replace
C123's later comparison against that old matrix with a complete original-DAG
to-native-row theorem. However, C123's40.49–47.07M selected binder cost is NOT
inside C97's fixed source base: removing that later comparison cannot be
subtracted from the base a second time.

The dominant remaining opportunities are a genuinely new full-kernel proof/
preparation program and/or a new complete original graph/support/source program.
Changing those algorithms requires independent ALL36 equality, source custody,
overflow/domain proofs, complete ownership and resource accounting. Lowering a
tariff for the same loop, omitting unselected-channel proof, assuming reuse of
a mutable preparation or borrowing a historical ledger is not such a change.

Before reconsidering a numerical source-birth run, a full whole/branch bound
must explicitly include the following presently unestablished replacement costs:

- Fresh expression/kernel authentication and complete original support/demand.
- Original output semantic powers from original summands and parent powers.
- Disjoint MAIN/radix/V/M slots and UIDs, including any deferred relocation.
- Complete source-derived incidence, changed quotient and local/radix inverse.
- Every emitted native coefficient, gauge, box, residual term and output row.
- Same-baseline full physical comparison, current reachable numeric/metadata
  ledgers, retained proof lifetimes and exact evidence publication.
- Native/runtime custody, value views, original-input reconstruction and later
  production progression gates where applicable.
