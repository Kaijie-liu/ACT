## Concrete source evidence

The remaining mandatory retirement/range checks alone establish a total-work

Every original owned row is canonical/sorted. After replacing only k positions
in a row of width w, the UNCHANGED subsequence is still sorted. C17 sorted all
w entries whenever the combined row ceased to be ordered, even if the k new
parent columns were themselves ordered. This structural fact permits an exact
sparse-update normalization primitive with no different HZ mathematics.

## Candidate two-stream construction

Keep the existing whole-row monotonic fast check. For a disordered updated row:

1. Separate unchanged columns/values U and replaced parent columns/cached exact
   product values V. The positions already exist in the complete alias hit
   records; do not rescan all predicates or recompute products.
2. U remains canonical and sorted. Check V's order; if necessary stably sort
   ONLY its k entries. Prove/preserve duplicate mapped-parent multiplicities.
3. Merge the two ordered streams. A vectorized exact placement is

       right_positions = searchsorted(U_columns, V_columns, side='right') + arange(k)

   These positions are strictly increasing even for duplicate V columns. Put
   V at those positions and U in their complement. Verify every position and
   output width; do not drop, round or combine coefficients before proof.
4. Group equal columns and use the SAME exact integer-grid collision sum.
   c10_alias_quotient_v1.exact_sum accumulates exact integer numerators on the
   fixed2^-72 grid, then checks final float64 representability/window. It is
   order-independent, so moving equal-column U terms before V terms does not
   weaken equivalence or change accepted final coefficient bits. Cancellation
   removes the same zero groups and the same ownership multiplicity deltas.

The binary/RHS maps, alias frontier/tags, global columns and reconstruction
ratios do not change. This is a representation-emission implementation, not
an input/phase split or a different solver path.
