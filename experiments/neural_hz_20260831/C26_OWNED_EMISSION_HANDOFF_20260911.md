## Ownership and proof are the first implementation obligation

Only fresh UNPUBLISHED generator-owned maps may be edited without a copy.
Do not pass a restored C25 Closed object's arrays to an in-place compiler,
pretend its old proof seal still validates, delete an incoming source or reuse
that old receipt after tags change. C24 Closed.validate fingerprints actual
source/HZ/maps/owners/slabs, so silently editing maps breaks its premise.

An explicit ownership transaction and a new composed proof type are needed.
The final type must distinguish ordinary rows, old aliases, unit reconstruction
and consumer redirects. No existing aliases/closed_uid_tables reader may treat
high positive tags as physical row indices. All readers and archive restoration
must agree on the new semantics and complete numeric ownership must be exposed.

One concrete option worth testing is REVERSIBLE lineage edits, so a new
proof can bind the original full-source certificate without retaining another
full pair of maps. C26 splice tags already retain original defining row, but
overwrite the old scale with h; redirect tags currently lose the old consumer
row. Thus C26 itself does NOT support such inversion. A NEW format would need
to retain both missing values (e.g. spare descriptor bits only if their complete
actual range is proved) and check byte-for-byte inversion of every edited field,
including old integer scale bits versus new float offset bits. No external source
may be mutated and no invisible registry array may hold the missing information.
Do not assume ranges or erase a copy charge until the copy operation is gone.
