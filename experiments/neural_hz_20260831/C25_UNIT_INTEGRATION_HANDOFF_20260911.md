## A concrete structural design candidate: transfer the row identity

For a proved eligible pair write

    D, UID u:  a*z + P = h
    C, UID v: -s*a*z + Q <= t       (or equality), s in {-1,+1}.

P uses continuous slots strictly below z, C begins with z and Q uses strictly
higher slots; D is binary-free, a is a positive power of two, z is output-dead
and has exactly these two predicate incidences. The C15 theorem gives

    R: s*P + Q <= t+s*h,    z = (h-P)/a.

The exact redundant-box proof and exact RHS sum are still REQUIRED, as are
independence of simultaneous selected pairs and noninterference with aliases.
All binaries and shared global dimensions remain. No sign-changing inequality
scaling or arbitrary numerical pivot is being suggested.

If R keeps the LARGE producer's UID u, rather than retaining consumer UID v,
P's many incidences need not be retagged: their row identity remains u.
Only Q's old v incidences change to u, while z's known pair incidences retire.
This algebraic ownership observation is a candidate for avoiding a parent-
width ownership-update pass. It is NOT yet an implemented or checked work
saving on the actual268 pairs. Events must be derived from canonical owned
rows, never subtracted merely because a packed count/sum happens to fit.

It creates a real mapping obligation: logical UID u may now live in an
inequality row, UID v is retired/redirected, and a retained consumer MAIN
definition can no longer assume its old own UID v. C24 closed_uid_tables and
eq_roots-to-own-UID assumptions cannot be reused unchanged. A new compact,
bidirectionally checked UID ownership/physical-row indirection or equivalent
typed row representation is necessary. This metadata and all relocation
queries must be priced and retained, not hidden in a proof registry. Packed
count/sum is not an arbitrary set-membership certificate.

A final-row writer would also have to consume the producer prefix directly
when constructing the new native phase, rather than first constructing C25's
complete old post-HZ and then copying its coefficients again. Existing native
construction work cannot simply be renamed to pay for extra copies. Show the
old/new operation and buffer lifetimes explicitly before claiming reuse.
No source/native cache may be discarded to fake whole-state reduction.
