## Mathematical contract

On the unchanged continuous relaxation P of the bound shared expert/router HZ,
write m=m0+Mz, b=q'Eb+c and d=q'(Ea−Eb). A closed slice [x,y] appends, in order,
Mz≤y−m0 and −Mz≤m0−x. These are rational overlays, never rounded into stored HZ
coefficients. All original constraints, full ten-class properties and shared/
private continuous/binary coordinates remain. Binaries are explicitly relaxed.

For original inequality/equality multipliers v≤0, eta, and slab multipliers p,q≤0,
the receiver independently reconstructs r=o−A'v−E'eta−pM+qM and
L=o0+rhs'v+eqrhs'eta+p(y−m0)+q(m0−x)−sum(abs(r)). It checks this exact lower
against the candidate. Endpoint objectives b+t*d and typed d,−d are distinct;
the latter do not include b or the property constant. Checked d/−d bounds can
later support strong slice-conditioned N1 ranges. Inverted ranges reject; no
cell is discarded without separately checked infeasibility evidence.

The externally fixed ordered closed grid covers the entire original factor-box
margin range. Every cell retains all endpoint obligations, including ties.
Its bound is the minimum of the checked local gate endpoint supports; the global
bound is the minimum across all cells. Zero-width margin creates one cell, not
necessarily one gate anchor: the unchanged sign/quarter-Lipschitz enclosure is
loose at nonzero points. A positive aggregate is conditional on the given HZ,
not a complete MoE certificate or route-reachability claim.
