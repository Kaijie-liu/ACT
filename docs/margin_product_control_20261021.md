Do not use old F0's normalized-variable or binary-column shifting convention.

closed-slice rows. The two externally bound typed d/−d certificates are rechecked
on this same P and cell. Intersect them with the original factor-box bounds;
inversion rejects rather than deleting an apparently infeasible cell.

`s*Dz+t*lambda−w≤s*(t−d0)` for(s,t)=(a,l),(b,h), and their negatives for

and its constant exactly once. Lambda has[a,b] bounds; w has the min/max of
the four rectangle corner products. No division is needed in degenerate cases.

independently checked original proportional constraint proves the margin sign s:
`−s*Mz/4+s*lambda≤s/2+s*m0/4` and `−s*lambda≤−s/2`. No sign premise means no
new relation rows, not an execution error. These are existing M3 relations,
not a new gate abstraction or a claim of exact sigmoid recovery.

The producer and checker construct the rows separately. Rounded copies enter
