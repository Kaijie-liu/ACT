## Primitive and proof

Given independently justified rational router bounds[l,u] within[-16,16],
sigmoid is monotone. Round l downward and u upward to24 fractional bits.
For each rounded endpoint x, repeatedly halve |x| until t<=1/2 (at most five
halvings). Let S be the Taylor sum for exp(t) through degree16. Positivity of
the terms gives S<=exp(t). The first omitted term is t^17/17!; subsequent
ratios are at most t/18, so

`exp(t) <= S + (t^17/17!)/(1-t/18)`.

Round this positive enclosure outward to40 fractional bits; square it with
outward rounding for each halving. For x>=0, exp(x)/(1+exp(x)) is increasing;
for x<0, 1/(1+exp(|x|)) is decreasing. Apply these rational maps to interval
endpoints and round outward to24 bits. No floating exponential is trusted.
The independent checker verifies the series, tail, every squaring, direction,
final range and externally supplied request/pair/support bindings. The producer
is not imported or called by the checker. Unsupported inputs fail closed.

This proves sigmoid enclosure **given margin evidence**. It does not validate
network-to-HZ, guard lowering, route exclusions, model quality or deployed
floating semantics. It never authorizes SAFE merely because a gate narrowed.
