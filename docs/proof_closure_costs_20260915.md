## Consequence for the next optional mode

An independent sign checker can exploit *available sufficient evidence* without
requiring an exact optimum; it cannot make a checked negative lower bound prove
safety. Record missing evidence, nonpositive evidence and conditional completion
as separate terminal outcomes. Charge loading, propagation, range proposals,
weighted construction/proposals, serialization and independent aggregation to
one deadline. Preserve the production optimal-status gate and all old results.
