## Why this particular check

A native MILP `mip_dual_bound` scalar is not enough to independently verify a
global bound. This study instead asks whether the continuous relaxation of a
regenerated **actual F0 HZ obligation** admits a checkable positive lower bound.
For finite-box LPs, signed inequality duals and arbitrary equality multipliers
give a bound after an exact residual minimization on the box. The checker
does not need primal optimality, zero duality gap, or even an optimal proposal;
its proof uses exact rational arithmetic on the supplied coefficients.

The existing proposer uses SciPy/HiGHS to obtain multipliers. It may require
solver completion to retrieve them; this is a proposer limitation, not the
mathematical checker's acceptance premise. A checked nonpositive lower bound
does not prove unsafety or that the root LP has a negative optimum. No raw
incumbent, missing bound or historical dual scalar can substitute for a proof.

## Frozen construction and limits

Use the original monolithic request's exact model/tensor/config identities,
CPU float64, one thread, no-support common-fact prelude, registered guarded
support and unchanged V2 allocation/25%/numerical settings. Regenerate the
request only up to its first `_build_disjunction` entry. Verify its pair and
property identity; snapshot the supplied scalar F0 HZ, export its continuous
LP and raise a dedicated capture-stop exception **before any weighted-property
MILP solve**. Routing and support calls before this capture are charged and
retained in the new journal. No complete request result is emitted.

Capture is externally capped at300s. A separate LP proposal has60s solver
limit and180s whole-process cap (including import, serialization and its local
rational check). Each of two fresh `python -S` independent checker processes
has300s cap. One control at a time; availability gate at least16GiB RAM,5GiB
disk, load/core <=0.5. No GPU or dependency installation. Stage errors or
timeouts stop this run and inventory unattempted controls; no retries/resume.
Full costs are stage-separated, not compared to the old300s verifier as speed.

The source has shared HZ factors; binary factors are explicitly relaxed to
[-1,1]. Existing independent HZ→LP checking reconstructs objective, affine
center, constraints and bounds, then checks signed multipliers and residuals
using `Fraction`. Two checker results must agree. The intended positive status
is `CHECKED_POSITIVE_SUPPLIED_F0_LP`, not SAFE; a complete MoE request still has
nine properties. The only positive threshold is the frozen1e-7.

## Trust boundary and nonclaims

This isolates the missing **downstream bound evidence**, deliberately retaining
the supplied F0 model. It still trusts network/input→HZ, guard/route lowering,
floating F0 construction and its association with the requested property.
It does not remove the F0-construction trust previously removed by the separate
MLP rational-McCormick study. That stronger path remains separate; the present
root-LP check is not a replacement for it or a regression in its claim.

Historical full V2 did not serialize these HZ/LP arrays. Regeneration uses the
same recipe but time-limited support can vary; coefficient equality with the
historical MILP cannot be claimed. A failed new LP does not isolate integer
reasoning as the unique cause. A positive one establishes a bound for the new
stored HZ, not retroactive justification of every old dual.

Production optimal-status acceptance remains unchanged. No historical TIMEOUT
is promoted. No source files in ACT or the frozen V2 protocol are edited.
