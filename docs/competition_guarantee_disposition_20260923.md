## 2. Guarantee: all 23 main gains are historical policy outcomes

The [source-applicability audit](main_table_source_applicability_20260921.md)
checks saved endpoint arithmetic and the frozen input-HZ formula, not recovered
historical hidden-layer matrices. For the 100-input main cohort:

- Exact requested rational-radius containment in materialized boxes fails on
  100/100 inputs. Interpreting epsilon as exact binary64 does not fix it.
- Materialized-box containment in the reconstructed HZ fails on 98/100 inputs,
  including all 23 gain model-input pairs.
- The [direct composition check](main_table_input_composition_20260921.md)
  confirms requested-to-HZ containment fails on all 100. Both inward and
  outward coordinates exist: these boxes are not nested. All 23 gains,
  covering 21 distinct input indices, are affected.

Tiny coordinate gaps are not a propagated output-error bound. Old positive
margins, shared code across arms, `exact=True`, numerical padding and hashes
cannot establish coverage of the missing inputs or their route obligations.
Conversely, failed enclosure is not an output counterexample: it proves
neither model unsafety nor that each old positive decision is factually false.

**Disposition:** keep 179/156/141, the 23 gains, losses and confidence intervals
as frozen-policy empirical accounting. Do not present them as certified
coverage of the requested real box or deployed float program. Do not deduct
23 from a historical count to manufacture a new experiment, or claim a
corrected-source run would retain the same ranking. Its result is unknown.
The main-table caption now carries this limitation next to the numbers.

## 3. The paper's actual claim and the gate for any upgrade

| Claim | Current disposition |
|---|---|
| Conditional complete-output decomposition, scoped reuse and relation-preserving obligations | Retained as mathematical rules with explicit coverage/enclosure/bound hypotheses |
| Main-cohort net numerical-policy gains versus registered internal configurations | Retained, restricted to the frozen objects, models and policy; not strict certificates |
| Shared-input ablation and complete conditional supplied-HZ proofs | Retained at their own evidence level; not a source proof for all main-table gains |
| ACT advantage on this MetaMoE author cohort | Not supported; report 4 versus 9 and zero ACT-only |
| Source-complete strict interpretation of the 23 main gains | Not supported; containment premise demonstrably fails |
| High-accuracy/cross-family route-changing strict certificates or completed native-float proof | Not established by these results |
| Submission/acceptance readiness | Not established; human technical review and clean-environment empirical reproduction remain separate open work |

The defensible paper is about **relation-preserving verification methods,
conditional guarantees and explicit evidence contracts**, with scoped empirical
benefits and adverse comparisons. It is not a completed source-to-deployment
strict certifier. Restricting claims does not guarantee publication or eliminate
the competitiveness risk; a human reviewer may judge the resulting scope
insufficient.

A stronger positive claim requires, on **one newly identified object**:

1. Exact declared input/domain semantics and checked outward containment.
2. Checked network/expert transformations, factor identity, guards and complete
   tie-legal route coverage (including justified exclusions).
3. All output obligations closed by applicable checked bounds, with reuse
   scopes and aggregation checked on those same matrices.
4. An explicit distinction between the declared real-valued graph and deployed
   floating execution. A real-graph proof does not silently acquire the latter.

This is an upgrade checklist, not a claim those checks already pass. Input
repair alone is insufficient. New matrices require new output evidence;
attaching old positive duals to repaired matrices is invalid. The old positive
input98 bundle and newer checked-source nonpositive construction remain
separate, and that sealed local search stays closed. Any future finite
same-object study needs its own scope/freeze rather than a retrospective
certificate relabeling.
