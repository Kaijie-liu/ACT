\section{S2a primary measurements and their limits}
\label{app:s2widths}
The compact source export in \path{paper/results/s2a_primary.json} binds the
accepted primary summary, all twenty model identities and the per-seed
measurements. \path{scripts/architecture_s2_figure.py} checks complete batch
coverage, aggregates with input-count weights and renders the figure without
loading a checkpoint or evaluating a network. Its source-bound export permits
rerendering without access to server-only evidence paths. This is reproducible
analysis of accepted records, not independent recomputation of their bounds.

Each model averages over all 10,000 inputs; arm summaries then average five
seeds. An expert-layer aggregate gives every expert equal weight, since all
experts have the same layer widths. These measurements share the full input
box and therefore do not isolate guard-conditioned or active-path behavior.
Hidden-unit rescaling and different learned logit scales prevent interpreting
raw width ratios as architecture-invariant precision ratios. An unstable-ReLU
fraction is a bound diagnostic, not a count of necessary branch-and-bound
queries. There is no causal layer ablation in this study.

One original incomplete D-act request failed cleanup and remains ERROR despite
a saved positive candidate. The dead-process parser was repaired without
changing numerical execution, and only previously unexecuted requests
continued in a separately bound executable. The old failure was neither rerun nor upgraded in the scientific queue. No clean-prediction difference

of such conflicts is not an independent proof of numerical soundness.

\subsection{Same-input regularizer contrasts across pipelines}
\label{app:sameinput}
A saved-record recount on the same first 1,000 inputs gives an IBP
regularizer contrast of $+0.28$ points, compared with $+0.20$ on the full
test set. CROWN-IBP gives $+2.78$ points on those first 1,000 inputs. The
same-input difference between the pipelines' regularizer contrasts is
$+2.50$ points, $[1.50,3.54]$. The pipelines differ in route reduction and
float32/float64 execution as well as bound computation, so this comparison
does not isolate a bound-method effect. CROWN-IBP gains and loses primary
IBP positives; neither pipeline dominates. The values and common-input
identities are retained in \path{paper/results/s2a_same_input_posthoc.json}.
