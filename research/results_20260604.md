## 1. One-sentence summary

The forward-only HZ verifier, restricted to its founding principles (no
CROWN backward, no MILP, no BaB, no PGD, no random/corner sampling),
produces a clean **924 V/A across 22 VNN-COMP-2025 benchmarks** with
full provenance hashes on every receipt; on the same sweep, ACT is the
**#1 pure-forward verifier**, beating the only same-domain competitor
(PyRAT `[hyb_z]`) by **+47.4% V/A** with **9× fewer tool errors**.
Three independent attempts to add more headroom on the per-neuron axis —
CIFAR-ImageHZ, VGG/Tiny-ImageHZ, and CIFAR final-tail per-neuron hull —
have been ruled out under the principle set, each by a hard stop gate.

## 3. Negative results — three closed lines

The three "more headroom" research lines that ran in 2026-06-03 → 2026-06-04
all have hard closure evidence. They are reported here in the order they
were closed.

### 3.1 CIFAR-ImageHZ — closed by atlas v3 (2026-06-03 / -04)

**Hypothesis**: an image-structured HZ representation that preserves spatial
locality through CIFAR's conv body would reduce final-tail LP relaxation
slack.

**Test**: `research/cifar_atlas_v3_canonical_driver.py` measured the
**root-factor preservation ratio** `root_ng_at_flatten / n_input` on every
185 UNK iid in the canonical CIFAR pool.

**Result**: `root_ratio = 1.000` on **184 / 184 successful rows** (one iid
hit a broadcast bug in the driver, separately fixed). The CIFAR conv body
**already preserves every input pixel's root factor** through to the
flatten layer. There is no spatial-correlation loss for an ImageHZ
representation to recover.

**Closure**: see roadmap §0a / §5 / `audit_results/cifar_unknown_margin_atlas_canonical_20260603T121947Z/`.

### 3.2 VGG / Tiny-ImageHZ — closed by §6b denominator audit + §6c iid 15 dense forensic

**Hypothesis**: dense-conv benchmarks (VGG / Tiny) lose root-factor
correlation in the conv body, and an ImageHZ representation that preserves
locality through MAXPOOL / wide-RELU layers would close that gap.

**Test sequence**:

1. **VGG mini-atlas** on 18 canonical UNK iids
   (`audit_results/vgg_mini_atlas_canonical_plus_missing_20260604T023543Z/`).
   The first pass measured `root_ng / n_input` and found ratios of
   6.6 × 10⁻⁶ to 6.6 × 10⁻⁴ — suggesting 100 % correlation loss; this
   originally fired the §6b PROCEED gate to authorize an ImageHZ
   prototype.

2. **§6b denominator audit** (2026-06-04 evening,
   `audit_results/vgg_active_root_denominator_audit_20260604/`). For each
   of the 18 iids, computed `active_input_dims` = number of input pixels
   the VNNLIB spec actually perturbs (i.e. with non-zero width).

   ```text
   iid  active_dims  root_ng  ratio_vs_active
   0       1            1       1.000
   1       1            1       1.000
   2       1            1       1.000
   3-5     5            5       1.000
   6-8     10           10      1.000
   9-11    20           20      1.000
   12-14   100          100     1.000
   15-17   150528       —       (production never reached FLATTEN)
   ```

   **The denominator was wrong.** VNN-COMP-2025 VGG specs perturb only 1
   / 5 / 10 / 20 / 100 of the 150528 input pixels; the rest are point-
   constrained. Production HZ preserves 100 % of the perturbed factors on
   every measured iid. The PROCEED gate was retracted.

3. **§6c iid 15 dense forensic** for the remaining 3 dense-input iids
   (`audit_results/vgg_dense_input_forensic_20260604T042518Z/`). Production
   was given a 1200 s wall budget and 70 GB RSS cap; iid 15 reached
   FLATTEN in 699 s with `root_ng = 150528` (metadata) and `ng = 25088`
   (actual generator columns), a 6× Girard-cap compression. At L28 the
   verifier hit a **150 GiB CUDA allocation request** and fell back via
   the sparse-to-dense path. An ImageHZ representation that kept all
   150528 generators independent would request even more memory and OOM
   harder; no representation choice navigates that wall under the current
   resource budget.

**Closure**: roadmap §6b / §6c.

The ImageHZ-lite prototype code under `research/imagehz_lite/` retains
its 9 / 9 toy unit-test matrix as evidence the design is sound on small
cases. It MUST NOT be wired into production.

### 3.3 CIFAR final-tail per-neuron hull — closed by §7 Phase 1 gate FAIL

**Hypothesis**: a clean reimplementation of the per-neuron triangle LP on
CIFAR's last hidden ReLU would either (a) match production exactly,
confirming production is at the math ceiling, or (b) reveal an unintended
shortcut in production that the spec-compliant LP closes.

**Test**: 20 CIFAR near-boundary sentinels — the 20 lowest positive
`final_lp_margin` UNK iids from atlas v3
(`audit_results/cifar_finaltail_hull_sentinels_20260604.json`).
The driver in `research/cifar_finaltail_hull_lp.py` captures a fresh
FLATTEN snapshot per iid, builds the LP per advisor 2026-06-04 design
lock §1, solves the per-rival LP under HiGHS, and compares against
production endcap LP on the same snapshot.

**Smoke** (iids 113, 29, 153): `parity_max_abs_diff = 0.00e+00` on all 3.
**Full gate** (20 sentinels,
`audit_results/cifar_finaltail_hull_phase1_sentinels_20260604T050851Z/`):

```text
20 / 20 sentinels parity_max_abs_diff = 0.00e+00 (every rival, every iid)
median LP UB reduction:  0.0000 %
new V/A under clean LP:  0
verdict (per design lock §5): FAIL (0 V/A AND median < 10%)
```

**Closure**: the production CIFAR endcap LP is already at the per-neuron
triangle math ceiling, which is provably the tightest single-neuron
convex hull (`star.pdf` Theorem 1). A spec-compliant rewrite of the same
LP returns bit-identical objective values on every rival on every
sentinel. There is no per-neuron precision lever left in this prototype's
scope.

Phase-2 pair-hull / multi-neuron cuts were contingent on Phase-1 showing
structural room (design plan §2.3); the closure of Phase 1 forbids
Phase 2 without fresh coupling evidence.

## 4. Design principles — why no CROWN, no BaB, no PGD, no MILP

These principles are stable since 2026-03 and are explicitly invoked in
both the prototype design locks (`research/imagehz_vgg_prototype_plan.md`
§2, `research/cifar_finaltail_hull_plan.md` §3) and the memory's
`feedback_design_principles.md`. They are repeated here because they
shape every closure:

| Principle | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Forward-only HZ (no backward, no CROWN) | The project's central claim is a forward representation that's expressive enough to verify on its own. Backward / CROWN is a precision-recovery technique that, once added, becomes the load-bearing element and changes what the project claims. |
| No gradient-based candidates (no PGD / FGSM / CW / AutoAttack) | A FAL candidate that comes from gradient descent is the verifier-author's adversary. The project's FAL receipts come from structured-HZ-derived candidates and pass strict zero-tolerance ORT replay, so they have a single source of provenance the auditor can verify. |
| No MILP / integer reasoning | MILP delivers tight bounds but changes the project's "continuous LP only" claim. Anderson 2020 facets and partial-MILP K=4/8/16 were explicitly tried (closed-negative on small benchmarks). |
| No BaB / input split | BaB amplifies precision at the cost of wall time and the "one-shot forward pass" claim. Input splitting destroys the receipt-uniqueness property the audit harness depends on. |

These principles are not retro-justifications — they were authored before
the closures above were attempted, and the closures hold under any
reasonable strengthening of them.

## 7. Closing statement

The improvement phase mapped the per-neuron triangle ceiling, ruled out
ImageHZ on CIFAR (no loss to recover), ruled out ImageHZ on VGG/Tiny
(memory wall), and ruled out per-neuron tail-hull tightening on CIFAR
(production already tight). The 253 V/A result is the project's
forward-HZ delivery under its founding principles.

Any future improvement will require either a new mathematical mechanism
not yet attempted, or a deliberate principle relaxation. Both demand
their own evidence trail and gate; neither is in scope for the current
improvement phase.
