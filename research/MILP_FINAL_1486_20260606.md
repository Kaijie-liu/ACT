## 4. Why last-layer MILP didn't crack acasxu / cifar

Last-layer MILP encodes EXACT ReLU only for the FINAL layer. Earlier
layers still use triangle relaxation in the walker's HZ closed-form
propagation.

For acasxu (5-6 sequential layers) and cifar (residual stacks), the
PHANTOM gap is distributed across all layers. Last-layer exactness
captures only a fraction.

**Solution: multi-layer MILP V2** (which I've implemented and validated
on Z0 toy to give 40.9% drop). To apply on real benches, need:
- Walker that produces FCHZState (with all SlackRecord layers)
- Currently we have PrunedState walker (streaming-prune, no records)

This is the next engineering step: ~1 week to write FCHZState walker
for the full ONNX op set we support.

---

## 5. Why cifar walker is too slow

Cifar walker takes 150-250s per iid even with 600s alarm. Hits DAG
safety which disables F1 capture.

For multi-layer MILP on cifar, two paths:
1. Faster walker (parallel conv, GPU)
2. Multi-layer MILP via FCHZState walker (parallel branches captured)

Both are multi-week engineering. Combined: 1-2 months to break cifar.
